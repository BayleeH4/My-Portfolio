## Audience and Purpose:

I want people who visit this site to understand who I am and what my future plans are. Those people who view it may be future employers interested in my previous work and education, or investors in the company I may currently be working for. Through this site, I want visitors to gain a clear understanding of my life as an accounting student, what my future goals may be in that field of work, and the skills/experiences that make me a strong candidate for future opportunities.
